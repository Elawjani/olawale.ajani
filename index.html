Case Study 1: AI Product Response Evaluation

Role: AI Evaluator & Quality Reviewer | Focus: Product judgment, usability, clarity, accuracy

Challenge

AI-generated responses need to be accurate, relevant, easy to understand, and aligned with user intent. Poor responses can create confusion, reduce trust, or fail to solve the user's problem.

Approach

  • Reviewed AI outputs across business, research, operations, customer experience, and compliance-related tasks.
  • Assessed whether responses matched the user’s likely intent and context.
  • Evaluated clarity, reasoning quality, usefulness, completeness, and user experience.
  • Provided structured feedback to identify issues and improve response quality.

Product Design Relevance

This work strengthened my ability to judge product quality, compare alternatives, identify friction in user experiences, and explain why one product decision better serves a user's need.

Case Study 2: Service User Journey Review

Role: Client Adviser | Focus: Service design, onboarding, support journeys, stakeholder coordination

Challenge

Users accessing complex support services often experience stress, uncertainty, and information overload. The service journey involves many touchpoints, agencies, documents, and support decisions.

Approach

  • Conducted needs assessments and support planning for vulnerable service users.
  • Reviewed communication touchpoints and identified moments where users needed clearer guidance.
  • Coordinated with public-sector, healthcare, legal, and community stakeholders.
  • Prepared reports and case documentation to support decision-making and service continuity.

UX Insight

Complex services work best when users understand what happens next, who is responsible, what information is needed, and how decisions affect them. Clear information architecture and predictable journeys improve trust.

Product Design Exercises

These are practice-led product design exercises that demonstrate how I evaluate user journeys and identify improvement opportunities. They can be expanded into visual mockups using Figma or another design tool.

Banking App Onboarding Review

Problem: New users may abandon account setup if the flow feels too long, unclear, or repetitive.

Suggested improvements: reduce steps, show progress indicators, use plain-language explanations, and separate required information from optional details.

AI Chatbot Help Experience

Problem: AI assistants can frustrate users when answers are vague, too long, or fail to address the real intent.

Suggested improvements: clarify user intent, structure answers, highlight next steps, and provide concise fallback options when confidence is low.

Government Support Service Journey

Problem: Service users can feel overwhelmed when navigating multi-agency processes.

Suggested improvements: create a clear timeline, define each stakeholder’s role, simplify documentation, and improve status updates.

Customer Feedback Dashboard Concept

Problem: Teams often collect feedback but struggle to convert it into product decisions.

Suggested improvements: group feedback by theme, severity, user segment, business impact, and recommended action.
